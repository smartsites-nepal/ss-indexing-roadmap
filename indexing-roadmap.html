📖 What is "Google Indexing", actually?

Think of Google Search like a giant library. Before a page of your site can show up when someone searches, three things have to happen, in order:

🔍

1. Crawl

Google's robot ("Googlebot") visits your page to see what's on it.

📚

2. Index

Google stores the page in its library. This is the step our tasks are about.

🏆

3. Rank

Google decides where the page appears in results. No index = no rank, ever.

A page can fail to get indexed for lots of reasons — Every "fix" in this quest is one of those reasons being corrected.
